# Forgeline Design Reference & Visual Language

1. **Type Scale**: Massive, condensed, industrial hierarchy from 120px+ display down to 10px micro-labels.
2. **Display Typography**: Big Shoulders Display (Black/900) set in uppercase with tight letter-spacing (-0.03em) dominating viewports.
3. **HUD Typography**: Chakra Petch & Oxanium for telemetry, status badges, and coordinate markers.
4. **Data & Monospace**: JetBrains Mono for system keys, UUID prefixes, timestamps, and commit hashes.
5. **Body Typography**: Barlow Semi Condensed for high-legibility dense tabular reads and metadata.
6. **Huge Type Deployment**: Full-bleed wordmarks acting as structural horizon anchors behind and through interactive surfaces.
7. **Spatial Rhythm**: Strict 4px/8px industrial grid with razor-sharp 1px dividers, zero pill rounding, and 45° chamfered corners.
8. **Surface Architecture**: Deep obsidian void (#060709) background layered with basalt (#0E1013) panels and steel (#2A2F37) borders.
9. **Accent Energy**: Plasma lime (#B8FF2C) for active/realtime signals; Ember orange (#FF5A1F) for forge heat & admin authority.
10. **State Accents**: Ice cyan (#5CE1E6) for sync & members; Amber (#FFB020) for review/warnings; Crimson (#FF2E4D) for 409 conflicts.
11. **Kinetic Motion**: Spring physics with snappy deceleration (GSAP/Motion), directional staggered reveals on enter.
12. **Fluid WebGL Integration**: Persistent canvas with viewport portal projection (<View>) preventing WebGL context resets.
13. **Displacement & Shaders**: Ambient HeatField noise background reacting to scroll velocity and cursor/gyro parallax.
14. **Depth & Shimmer**: Ember particle sparks ascending from the bottom viewport edge behind high-contrast HUD cards.
15. **Mobile-First Ergonomics**: Bottom 64px thumb-zone navigation, 44px+ touch targets, and tactile haptic response triggers.
